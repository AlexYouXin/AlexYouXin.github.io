I received my Ph.D. degree from the Institute of Medical Robotics, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I was supervised by Prof. [Yun Gu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=0pX32mkAAAAJ&hl=en) and Prof. [Jie Yang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=tmx7tu8AAAAJ&hl=en).

I was also a visiting scholar at the Chair for Computer Aided Medical Procedures & Augmented Reality, Technical University of Munich, where I worked with Prof. [Nassir Navab](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kzoVUPYAAAAJ&hl=de) on anatomical motion modeling. I have had the privilege to collaborate with Prof. [Shaohua Kevin Zhou](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=8eNm2GMAAAAJ&hl=en) on histology image analysis. Prior to this, I interned at GMAI center, Shanghai AI Laboratory, working with Prof. [Junjun He](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Z4LgebkAAAAJ&hl=en) on foundation models for medical image segmentation.

My research advances **AI for medicine and healthcare** through generative and agentic AI, multimodal learning, and world models. Ultimately, I aim to build virtual patients — faithful, dynamic digital twins that can support preoperative planning and intraoperative navigation in real-world clinical settings.

**Note: I'm always open to academic collaborations and discussions. Please feel free to reach out by sjtu_youxin [at] sjtu [dot] edu [dot] cn.**
