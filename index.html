Let's goooooooooooo!

I received my Ph.D. degree from the Institute of Medical Robotics, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I was supervised by Prof. Yun Gu and Prof. Jie Yang.

I was also a visiting scholar at the Chair for Computer Aided Medical Procedures & Augmented Reality, Technical University of Munich, where I worked with Prof. Nassir Navab on anatomical motion modeling. I have had the privilege to collaborate with Prof. Shaohua Kevin Zhou on histology image analysis. Prior to this, I interned at GMAI center, Shanghai AI Laboratory, working with Prof. Junjun He on foundation models for medical image segmentation.

My research advances AI for medicine and healthcare through generative and agentic AI, multimodal learning, and world models. Ultimately, I aim to build virtual patients — faithful, dynamic digital twins that can support preoperative planning and intraoperative navigation in real-world clinical settings.

Note: I’m always open to academic collaborations and discussions. Please feel free to reach out by sjtu_youxin [at] sjtu [dot] edu [dot] cn.

research topics

  • Multimodal & Agentic AI: Multi-modal representation learning, foundation models and agentic AI that reason across modalities and clinical context.
  • Generative Medical AI: Diffusion-based and Flow matching-based generative models for 3D and 4D medical data.
  • Shape & Topology Representation Learning: Learning with shape priors and topological constraints for anatomical shapes.
  • Clinically Grounded AI: Algorithms tied to clinically meaningful structures and workflows, developed through expert challenge collaborations on cardiac, vascular and airway anatomy.
  • World Modeling for Virtual Patients: Dynamic digital twins and generative world models that simulate patient-specific anatomy and physiology for preoperative planning and intraoperative navigation.
